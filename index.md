## Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Updated / 更新：2026-10-17

应用无需账号，不含广告、第三方统计或追踪，不向开发者服务器传输个人数据。进度、最佳用时、教学状态及语言偏好保存在设备本地；删除应用可清除本地数据，备份由 Apple 系统设置管理。通过 Apple StoreKit 获取的商店地区仅用于选择默认语言，不会发送给开发者，语言可手动修改。

The app requires no account and contains no ads, third-party analytics or tracking. It does not transmit personal data to developer servers. Progress, best times, tutorial status and language preferences are stored locally. Deleting the app clears local data; backups are controlled by Apple system settings. Storefront region from Apple StoreKit is used on-device to choose a default language, is not sent to the developer, and you can change the language manually.
